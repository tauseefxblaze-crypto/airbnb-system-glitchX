Booking a stay

Every StayNest home is visited in person by our local team before it goes live. We check the rooms, bathrooms, heating and backup power, and the photos you see were taken during that visit.

Most hosts welcome guests from 2:00 pm and ask you to check out by 11:00 am. The exact times are on each listing, and many mountain stays offer self check-in for late arrivals.

Children are welcome almost everywhere, and many homes provide a cot on request. Pets depend on the host, so look for "Pets allowed" in the listing's house rules.

Pakistani citizens don't need one for Hunza, Skardu or Swat. Some border areas need a permit for foreign visitors. Your host will tell you if your route needs any paperwork.

Payments

You can pay with Visa or Mastercard debit and credit cards, JazzCash, Easypaisa or bank transfer. Some hosts also accept cash on arrival, which is marked on their listing.

No. The total on the booking screen includes the nightly price, the cleaning fee and our 8% service fee. You won't see a single extra rupee at check-out.

A PDF receipt is emailed as soon as your stay ends. Need your company name on it? Send us your booking reference and we'll reissue it within a day.

Cancellations and refunds

Go to your trips, choose the booking and tap "Cancel". You'll see exactly how much will be refunded before you confirm.

Wallet refunds (JazzCash, Easypaisa) arrive within 24 hours. Card refunds take 5 to 10 working days depending on your bank.

If a landslide or snowfall closes the official route to your stay, you get a full refund or free date change, whatever the cancellation policy says.

Hosting

We send your payout every Monday for guests who checked in the week before, to your bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa. You keep 97% of the booking.
