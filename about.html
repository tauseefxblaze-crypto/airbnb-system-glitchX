About StayNest

We think the best part of a trip is who you meet along the way.

Our mission

Make travel in Pakistan feel like staying with a friend

Pakistan is full of extraordinary places and even more extraordinary hosts. But finding a clean room with a reliable heater, a fair price and someone who picks up the phone used to take a dozen calls and a lot of luck.

StayNest brings those homes together in one place. We visit every listing, show the full price upfront, and keep a real support team on call, so guests can explore with confidence and families can earn from the homes they love.

Our story

From one spare room to 38 cities

  1. 2021

    A spreadsheet in Lahore

    Our founders kept a shared list of trusted guest houses for friends heading north. It hit 300 entries in a month.

  2. 2022

    The first 100 homes

    We drove the Karakoram Highway visiting hosts one by one, from Gilgit to Khunjerab, and took every photo ourselves.

  3. 2024

    Experiences launch

    Hosts asked to share more than beds. Food walks, jeep safaris and craft workshops joined the site.

  4. 2026

    120,000 guests and counting

    Today StayNest covers 38 destinations, with a support team in Lahore, Islamabad and Gilgit.

What we believe

Three promises we keep

Seen with our own eyes

No listing goes live until someone from our team has stood in it, slept in it if we can, and checked the backup power.

Honest prices

The number you see is the number you pay. Fees are shown before you book, always.

Hosts first

Our hosts keep 97% of every booking. When they do well, the places you love stay open.
