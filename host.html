How it works

Three steps to your first guest

  1. Tell us about your place

    Add a few photos, the number of beds and what makes it special. It takes about 15 minutes, and you can save as you go.

  2. We visit and help you shine

    A local scout drops by within a week to check the basics and, if you like, shoot professional photos for free.

  3. Welcome guests, get paid

    Accept the bookings that suit you. Money reaches your bank account or JazzCash every Monday after check-in.
