When to go where

Pakistan has a season for everything. Here's our quick guide so you don't end up in Skardu in January by accident.

Spring

March – May

Cherry and apricot blossoms in Hunza and Skardu. Lahore is warm but still pleasant in the evenings.

Summer

June – August

Prime time for the north: Naran, Kalam and Deosai open up. Skip the plains, it's above 40°C.

Autumn

September – November

Golden poplars and clear skies in the valleys. Our favourite time for photography trips.

Winter

December – February

Snow in Murree, festivals in Lahore and perfect desert weather in Cholistan and Karachi.
