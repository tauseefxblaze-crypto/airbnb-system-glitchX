About this place

Wake up to sunrise over Rakaposhi from a cabin made almost entirely of glass. Ayesha's family built it on the edge of their apricot orchard, a ten-minute walk from Baltit Fort and the cafés of Karimabad.

Inside you'll find a wood stove for chilly evenings, a small kitchen with a proper chai pan, and a reading nook with a view of the whole valley. Breakfast of fresh bread, local honey and apricot jam is brought to your door every morning.

The cabin has solar backup, so the lights and Wi-Fi stay on during load-shedding. The road from Karakoram Highway is paved all the way to the gate.
